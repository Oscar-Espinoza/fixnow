## Recommendation

**Top exact-model candidate: Ilywkins' free iPhone 18 Pro.** Its CC BY 4.0 terms support a commercial client website and modification, with attribution we can meet. It is a research lead, **not yet an implementation-ready asset**: source format, byte size, textures and separate objects need inspection through an authorized existing download account. If that fails, use Ranguel's free **17 Pro** as the requested fallback. Neither currently demonstrates the internal layers needed for the existing exploded animation.

If Oscar accepts **18 Pro Max** instead, the inspected compact `handset.glb` is the most practical web exterior: free, ~523 KiB and 13.9k triangles. It is already flattened to one mesh, so use it for rotation/realistic exterior presentation, not as a five-layer teardown. Do not relabel it as an 18 Pro. For genuine modeled internals, the $89 Render-Edge model is the documented option, best used to generate optimized rendered images/video; it was not purchased or downloaded.

## License details that affect implementation

- [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits commercial adaptation and redistribution. Add a visible model credit or linked credits page naming the creator, linking the source and license, and describing modifications; retain supplied notices. For the compact derivative, preserve the NOTICE's existing modification history too. Attribution is feasible; these are not CC BY-NC or CC BY-ND assets.
- [ArtStation stock-asset EULA §§4(c), 5](https://www.artstation.com/marketplace-product-eula) allows modification and contractor/client sharing for the licensed work, subject to restrictions. The Standard license's **20,000 limit is monthly views**, although product cards omit “monthly.” Rendered output is the clearer route; confirm permission for a publicly retrievable standalone GLB before live 3D use. Extended license availability/pricing for these two products was not established.
- [CGTrader terms §§21A–21B](https://www.cgtrader.com/pages/terms-and-conditions) permit commercial modification but require incorporation and commercially reasonable access protection. A public `/assets/model.glb` is readily retrievable; royalty-free does not automatically authorize that delivery. No AI restricts ML/training, not ordinary website rendering.
- These licenses cover the modeller's licensed work, not automatically every Apple trademark, UI screenshot or third-party texture. Use our own screen artwork, avoid implied Apple endorsement, and verify included texture rights before shipping. This is why a downloadable file alone is not full rights clearance.

Rejected: [iMeshh 17 Pro](https://imeshh.com/assets/iphone-17-pro) and [TurboSquid 18 Pro pack ($15)](https://www.turbosquid.com/3d-models/3d-iphone-18-pro-all-colors-3d-model-obj-fbx-glb-usdz-blend-2624918) explicitly say **editorial only**. The [3dsky 18 Pro ($7, 32 MB, 65,244 polys)](https://3dsky.org/3dmodels/show/apple-iphone-18-pro-smartphone) is cheaper, but its [customer terms §10](https://3dsky.org/faq/115/show) restrict 3D client distribution/access; not a straightforward public-GLB option. No free 18/17 model with documented separated internal components was verified in this search.
